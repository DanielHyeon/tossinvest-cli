## Comparison and uncertainty

Preserve the full input universe with IDs, per-filter true/false/unknown, candidate invalid/excluded/unfilled, fixed-arm censored/invalid/closed, rejection reasons and session counts. False selection is known no-trade contribution zero, not a filled trade with zero risk. Unknown filter or unresolved baseline gives unknown paired difference, never zero. Conditional executed-trade mean R is a separate descriptive metric with its own count.

Use a common complete cohort across all three filter comparisons and both base/stress scenarios for comparable summaries. A market/date session participates only if every submitted candidate in it has all three known filters and closed valid baseline in both scenarios. Publish contributing and excluded candidate/session IDs, reasons, full-universe missingness and trade-count reduction. Stress uses its own positive simulated risk; do not interpret stress R difference as constant-denominator account return.
