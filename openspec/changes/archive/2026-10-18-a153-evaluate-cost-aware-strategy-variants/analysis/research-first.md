## Evaluator cohort and comparison constraints

The unchanged a151 evaluator requires the ATR 15-bar warmup and valid fixed, structural and ATR geometries even if the analysis ultimately reports only the fixed-stop outcome. Reuse therefore means an explicitly restricted **a151-compatible, all-three-valid candidate universe**; report each upstream exclusion and its denominator. It cannot represent the larger population that would qualify for a fixed stop alone. Factoring a fixed-only evaluator would require a distinct, independently validated behavior contract; copying or silently modifying the sealed evaluator is not recommended for the bounded scope.

Each market and filter should publish candidate IDs and counts for its selected, unselected, unknown, filled and censored sets. A per-filter paired cohort can measure that filter against its own baseline but comparisons across filters on different complete-case cohorts are not rankings. Any cross-filter comparison requires an explicitly identified common, fully observable paired cohort and its attrition; retain the original universe and per-filter results alongside it. Define the cluster-bootstrap resampling unit, seed, iteration count and percentile interpolation rule in the approved design before computation; a library default must not silently determine confidence bounds.
