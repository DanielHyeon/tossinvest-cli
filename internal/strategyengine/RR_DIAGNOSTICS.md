# Parker RR diagnostics

`ParkerRRFromDecision` reads a caller-supplied `DecisionRecord` without changing the frozen v1 decision or its identity. Space RR is forward obstacle distance divided by planned entry minus initial stop; target RR is target distance divided by the same planned **per-share** risk. With entry 100, stop 99.3, forward space 1.2 and target 102.1, these are 12/7 and 3 respectively. A 3R target guarantees neither unobstructed space nor realized profit. The existing `RR_GE_2` reason remains a raw compatibility label, not evidence of a 2R entry floor: frozen v1 retains its existing 1.2% space and 1.5 RR predicates.

`ParkerRealizedRR` separately uses **filled account-currency risk**: (final actual average entry minus initial protective stop) × final filled quantity × entry-time FX rate. `GrossPnL` and `Costs` are final account-currency amounts; costs must be nonnegative, including explicit zero. The result is (gross PnL minus costs) / filled account risk. Negative or zero net PnL is valid. Entry price, stop and rate are snapshots fixed at entry, not current prices or a recomputed stop. `EntryAt` identifies the entry snapshot; `FXAt` is the entry snapshot freeze instant, not the exchange quote timestamp; it must match `EntryAt`. A different snapshot is invalid. `FXRate` converts one unit of `FXFrom` price currency into `FXTo` account currency. Same-currency FX must be 1. The caller must supply entry, exit and cost finality and provenance; missing evidence yields unknown, invalid or mismatched values yield invalid. Exact rational strings preserve precision.

All inputs and provenance are **caller-supplied and unverified**. This pure calculation does not authenticate evidence, fetch fills or costs, reconcile a ledger, approve trades, or connect to runtime. Planned and filled denominators have different units and may differ due to execution; compare them only with their labels and units. No live collection or integration is implied.
