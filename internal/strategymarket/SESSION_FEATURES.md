# Session feature calculations

`CalculateSessionFeatures` is an offline pure calculator; it does not read a calendar or authenticate a provider, fetch bars, mint official source seals or feed the frozen Parker runtime. All session, calendar, source, timestamps and bar values are caller-supplied and unverified. The caller must supply an explicitly verified KR calendar assertion, version, trading date and session bounds; the calculator checks internal consistency but does not determine holidays or attest authenticity. US is unsupported. Accepted KR sessions span at most 390 minutes, in complete five-minute intervals, with KRW and unadjusted official-source-labelled bars. Numeric fields are bounded to 64 characters and computed exactly.

Close labels represent `[t-1m,t)` and `[t-5m,t)`. Each bar must be closed and received by `AsOf`, and each supplied five-minute OHLCV must equal its five constituent one-minute bars. Ordered, contiguous prefixes from open through the last completed minute are required. Gaps or missing evidence do not yield a ready indicator; malformed, duplicated, reversed, future, mixed, adjusted or inconsistent bars are invalid. No missing bars, timestamps or calendar facts are inferred.

`Method` is a typical-price **approximation**, not trade-level VWAP: cumulative sum of `(high+low+close)/3 × volume` divided by cumulative volume. Zero-volume bars do not contribute; zero total volume is not ready. `EMA9` uses nine complete five-minute closes as its SMA seed, then alpha 1/5; it resets per supplied session. `Slope` is percent change between the VWAPs at the two latest complete five-minute boundaries. At 09:46 these are 09:40 and 09:45, not 09:46. Values are exact rational strings; consumers may round only for display. The result labels each method, calendar/session identity, ranges, counts, latest receipt time and canonical input digest. The top-level `known` status requires all three values to be ready; individual values retain their own states even when the overall result is not ready. A missing five-minute prefix can leave VWAP ready while EMA and slope remain not ready. These results grant no trading authority.
