# Prospective MFI shadow evaluation (offline)

This tool does not collect data, call a model, schedule work, place orders, or modify r1. New observations require separate authorization. Input files, captures, registration time and session status are supplied evidence; hashes and self-consistency do not prove their authenticity or independently prove preregistration. Results are observational and always inconclusive rather than a trading or performance approval.

`analysis.py register DRAFT MANIFEST` creates an exclusive manifest outside the repository. Draft JSON must contain `first_observation_not_before` (offset-bearing future timestamp), `session_plan` with explicit `KR` and `US` arrays of `{date, open_at, close_at, cadence_seconds, max_cycle_skew_seconds}`, `coverage_thresholds` with numeric `min_cycle_fraction`, `min_mfi_fraction`, `min_label_fraction` in [0,1], `sources` with `KR` and `US` objects `{name, timezone}`, the 60-minute `horizon_minutes`, finite nonnegative `max_bar_age_seconds` and `max_label_delay_seconds`, and nonempty strings for `completeness_rules`, `candidate_rule`, `cycle_schedule`, `interruption_rules`, `replacement_rules`, `interim_reporting_rules`, `stop_rules`, `analysis_version`. The tool stamps creation time, but external preservation is required to establish the time and immutability of registration. The five-sessions-per-market target is exploratory and not automatically asserted complete.

`analysis.py record SNAPSHOT MANIFEST EVENT SIDECAR` appends a JSON event to a separate sidecar, binding its first event to the manifest SHA-256. A capture event has `kind: capture`, `state_digest`, `capture_digest`; a session event has `kind: session`, `market`, `date`, `status` (`complete`, `partial`, `failed`, `unknown`) and a reason for partial/failed. Duplicate identities and manifest changes are refused. Record paths must not overlap inputs or repository. Writes refuse symlinks, hard links, and overwrite; preserve the resulting files externally. A sidecar header is an integrity check, not external attestation; coordinated replacement of both manifest and sidecar is not detectable by this check alone.

`analysis.py SNAPSHOT MANIFEST SIDECAR OUTPUT` evaluates a local snapshot, creating a new output file. SNAPSHOT contains `ledger.jsonl`, `states/<sha256>.json` (existing a145 format) and `captures/<sha256>.json` (digest of complete capture bytes). A capture has `state_digest`, `market`, `symbol`, `observed_at`, `source`, `source_timezone`, `received_at`, and `bars` with offset-bearing `close_at`, `open`, `high`, `low`, `close`, `volume`. The capture must match original state bars and registered source, be received by judgment time, and include no bar after receipt. This checks supplied evidence, not the authenticity of its source or clock. A missing capture is excluded. Historical records predating the manifest are refused; no historical offsets are inferred.

Outputs include candidate denominators by market/session/selection, MFI and label exclusion reasons, fixed 60-minute mean and strictly-positive fraction by direction, same-cycle equal-weight rising/falling differences, and planned versus observed cycle/coverage summaries. A `complete` session supplied by a caller is never proof of full capture: eligibility only means supplied status and registered coverage thresholds were met. Unknown or invalid selection remains unknown. Results are not account return, causality, a same-K comparison, or proof of an MFI strategy; observations can overlap and repeat symbols. Candidate-pool completeness requires independent evidence and is not validated by the free-text pool rule.
